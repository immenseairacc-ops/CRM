Immense CRM — WhatsApp & Mail Tab Switcher (Chrome Extension)
==============================================================

Install Steps (30 seconds per laptop):
1. Open Google Chrome and go to: chrome://extensions
2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right toggle).
3. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left) and select this folder:
   D:\sale-crm\chrome-extension
4. Refresh your Immense CRM tab.

How it works:
- Click "Send on WhatsApp" in CRM -> Chrome switches directly to your ALREADY-OPEN
  WhatsApp Web tab and loads the client chat + quotation text inside that SAME tab
  (no 2nd WhatsApp tab).
- Click "Open Hostinger Mail Tab" in CRM -> Chrome switches directly to your
  ALREADY-OPEN Hostinger Mail tab without opening a 2nd login tab.
